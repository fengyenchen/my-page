馮妍禎 Yen-Chen, Feng

Yen-Chen Feng, born in 2005 in Taoyuan, Taiwan, is currently pursuing her studies at National Taiwan University. She is a passionate observer dedicated to discovering meaning within the subtleties of daily life and seeking impactful ways to convey profound messages to the world.

Driven by a keen eye for detail, Yen-Chen does not confine herself to a single framework of expression. She embraces challenges and finds joy in exploring the unknown, viewing every step out of her comfort zone as an opportunity to broaden her creative boundaries. For her, the ultimate value lies in fostering meaningful connections through sincere communication and creativity.

Drawing and multimedia are the primary mediums through which Yen-Chen dialogues with the world. To her, drawing is more than a hobby—it is a process of visual thinking. By capturing thoughts through spontaneous lines and appreciating diverse forms of media, she continuously refines her perception, learning to translate abstract concepts into visual narratives with warmth and depth.




>  Capturing the essence of thoughts through mindful observation and creative expression.
