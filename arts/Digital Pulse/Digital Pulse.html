Digital Pulse

Dynamic digits and geometric brickwork create a rhythmic visual.
跳動數字與幾何磚瓦的色彩律動。

This dynamic video piece centers around the lively movement and transformation of numbers. Various digits are presented visually in an animated fashion, appearing, changing, and shifting across the screen. The background cleverly utilizes red and orange brick-like or checkerboard textures, which emerge with wavy or jagged edges. These textured elements add a rich visual layer and dynamic energy to the clean outlines of the numbers. The work combines minimalist numerical aesthetics with a rhythmic background design, creating an engaging visual cadence.
這是一個動態的影片作品，以數字的跳動和變化為核心。畫面中，各種數字在視覺上以活潑的方式呈現，時而替換、時而移動。背景則巧妙地運用了紅色和橘色的磚塊或棋盤格紋理，這些紋理以波浪狀或鋸齒狀的邊緣出現，為簡潔的數字線條增添了豐富的視覺層次感和動態能量。作品結合了簡約的數字美學和富有節奏感的背景設計，創造出一種引人入勝的視覺韻律。
